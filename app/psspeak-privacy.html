PS Speak – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

PS Speak is a voice interpreter app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the developer of PS Speak collects nothing. There is no PS Speak server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Your Gemini API key

PS Speak works with your own Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. The key is stored only on your phone, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. It is sent only to Google's Gemini API, to authorise your requests. It is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Speech and text you translate

When you use Conversation or Listen, audio from your microphone is streamed directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API for translation. When you use Type, the text you type is sent to Google's Gemini API for translation and speech. Nothing passes through the developer. Audio is not stored on your phone.

Google processes this data under your own Google account and Google's terms, including the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. On Google's free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products; on the paid tier, it does not. See Google's terms for details.

History on your phone

If "Save conversations on this phone" is on (Settings), finished sessions are saved as text in a database on your phone only. You can delete one session, delete all, turn saving off, or set automatic deletion after 7, 30 or 90 days. History is excluded from cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to hear speech for translation, only while a session is running.
  • Internet: to reach Google's Gemini API.
  • Foreground service (microphone) and notifications: to keep a translation session running with the screen off, shown by an ongoing notification with a Stop button.

Children

PS Speak is intended for adults. Google requires Gemini API users to be 18 or older.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
