Summary

The တရားနာယူ ("Tayar Nar Yu") app — the Android app and the website dhamma-mp3.pyisoe.com — does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in. No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Listening history, favourites, resume positions and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes them.
  • Talks you choose to download (⬇) are saved to the Download/Dhamma folder on your device.

Network connections

Audio streams directly from dhammadownload.com, which permits direct linking to its files. The talk catalogue is loaded from dhamma-mp3.pyisoe.com, hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, those servers may log technical data such as your IP address under their own policies. We receive no data from them.

Android permissions

  • Internet — to stream talks.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock — to keep a talk playing while the screen is off and to show playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
