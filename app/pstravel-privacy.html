PS Travel – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

PS Travel is a travel helper app published by PYI SOE LTD. It reads and explains signs, menus and labels you photograph, answers travel questions and helps you get places. This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: the developer of PS Travel collects nothing. There is no PS Travel server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Your Gemini API key

PS Travel works with your own Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. The key is stored only on your phone, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. It is sent only to Google's Gemini API, to authorise your requests. It is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Photos, questions and voice

When you read a photo on the Look tab, the photo (made smaller first) is sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API to be read, translated and explained. When you use the Ask tab, your typed questions, your recorded voice questions and any photo you attach are sent to Google's Gemini API in the same way. Answers are read aloud using Google's Gemini speech service. Nothing passes through the developer. Voice recordings are not stored on your phone; only the text of what you said is kept in History.

Google processes this data under your own Google account and Google's terms, including the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. On Google's free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products; on the paid tier, it does not. See Google's terms for details.

Location (optional)

Location is off until you turn it on in the Ask tab. When it is on, PS Travel uses your phone's approximate (not precise) location while the app is open, to know which city you are in. The city name and approximate coordinates are sent to Google's Gemini API with your questions, so answers fit where you are, and the city is saved with your scans and chats in History. You can turn location off at any time.

Smart search (optional)

Settings has two switches, off by default: Smart nearby search and Smart web search. When they are on, Google's Gemini API may look up places in Google Maps (using your approximate location, if location is on) or search Google to answer a question. These lookups are made by Google as part of your Gemini request, under Google's terms; Google may keep these requests for a limited time as its terms describe. Sources are shown under the answer and open in Google Maps or your browser.

Google Maps

Navigate and "Search in Google Maps" open the Google Maps app or website with the place or search you chose. What happens there is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

History and saved places on your phone

If "Save history" is on (Settings), the photos you read, their explanations, your chats and the time and city are saved on your phone only. Places you save are also kept on your phone so you can show them to a driver without internet. You can delete any item, clear all history, or turn saving off. History and saved places are excluded from cloud backup, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Reporting an answer

If an answer is wrong or offensive, you can tap "Report". This opens an email in your own email app, pre-filled with the answer and the question. No photo is attached, and nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you send it, the developer receives your email address and whatever the email contains, uses them only to look into the report and reply to you, and does not share them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the photo you want read, only while the Look tab is open.
  • Microphone: to record a spoken question, only while you hold the record button.
  • Approximate location (optional): to know your city, only when you turn it on.
  • Internet: to reach Google's Gemini API.

Photos you pick from your gallery are chosen with Android's photo picker; PS Travel cannot see the rest of your photos.

Children

PS Travel is intended for adults. Google requires Gemini API users to be 18 or older.
