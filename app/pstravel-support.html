PS Travel – Support

PS Travel is a travel helper for Android, published by PYI SOE LTD. Point your phone at a sign, menu or label and it tells you what it says and what it means, in your language, out loud. Ask travel questions, show a phrase to a local person, and get places with Google Maps. It uses Google's Gemini API with your own Gemini API key.

Getting started

How do I get a Gemini API key?

Open PS Travel and tap "Open Google AI Studio". Sign in with your Google Account, tap "Create API key", copy the key, come back to PS Travel, paste it and tap "Check and save key". Creating a key is free.

What does it cost?

You buy PS Travel once. Reading, answers and speech run on your own Gemini API key, so any API charges come from Google, not from us. Most people can start on Google's free tier with no card. If you turn on billing in Google AI Studio, Google charges you directly for what you use. A Gemini app subscription (Google AI Pro or Ultra) does not include API use.

What are the Smart search switches?

Settings has Smart nearby search (real places from Google Maps) and Smart web search (fresh facts from Google Search, such as opening hours). They need billing turned on for your key, and Google may charge for them, so they are off by default. Without them, PS Travel still answers and can open the Google Maps app for you.

Common problems

"Your Gemini key has used up its daily limit"

Free-tier keys allow a limited number of requests per day. Wait until the time shown, or turn on billing in Google AI Studio.

"Your Gemini key has reached its per-minute limit"

Too many requests in a short time. Wait a minute and try again.

"That key didn't work"

Check you copied the whole key. If it worked before, it may have been deleted, changed or restricted in Google AI Studio: create a new key and replace it in PS Travel's Settings.

"Gemini isn't available in your region yet"

Google offers the Gemini API only in certain countries. PS Travel works only where Google offers it; see Google's list of available regions.

"Smart search needs billing turned on"

Smart nearby search and Smart web search do not work on free-tier keys. Turn on billing in Google AI Studio, or switch Smart search off in Settings.

The photo can't be read

Move closer, hold the phone steady and make sure there is enough light. Very small or blurry text may not be readable.

The camera, microphone or location doesn't work

On Android: Settings → Apps → PS Travel → Permissions, and allow Camera, Microphone or Location.

An answer was wrong or offensive

Tap "Report" under the answer. This opens a pre-filled email in your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it. Answers come from Google's Gemini models, so check important information, such as allergens, opening hours or medical instructions, with the place itself or a qualified person.

Your data

We collect nothing: no account, no analytics, no ads. Your key, History and saved places stay on your phone. Photos, questions and (if you turn it on) your approximate location go only to Google's Gemini API. To delete History, open Settings and tap Clear history, or delete single items in History.
