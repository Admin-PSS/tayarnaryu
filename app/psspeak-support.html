PS Speak – Support

PS Speak is a voice interpreter for Android and iPhone, published by PYI SOE LTD. It translates speech between two people, out loud and as text, using Google's Gemini API and your own Gemini API key.

Getting started

How do I get a Gemini API key?

Open PS Speak and tap "Open Google AI Studio". Sign in with your Google Account, tap "Create API key", copy the key, come back to PS Speak, paste it and tap "Check and save key". Creating a key is free.

What does it cost?

You buy PS Speak once. Translation runs on your own Gemini API key, so any API charges come from Google, not from us. Most people can start on Google's free tier with no card. If you turn on billing in Google AI Studio, Google charges you directly for what you use; PS Speak shows an estimate and can warn you at a monthly budget you set in Settings. A Gemini app subscription (Google AI Pro or Ultra) does not include API use.

Common problems

"Google's Gemini is busy right now"

Google's servers are under heavy load. Wait a moment and try again. This happens more often with free-tier keys; a key with billing turned on is more reliable.

"You've reached the free limit for now"

Your key has used its free-tier allowance for the moment. Wait a minute, or turn on billing in Google AI Studio.

"Your Gemini key isn't working anymore"

The key was deleted, changed or restricted in Google AI Studio. Create a new key and replace it in PS Speak's Settings.

"Gemini translation isn't available in your region"

Google offers the Gemini API only in certain countries. PS Speak works only where Google offers it; see Google's list of available regions.

The app can't hear me

PS Speak needs microphone access. On iPhone: Settings app → PS Speak → Microphone. On Android: Settings → Apps → PS Speak → Permissions → Microphone. Close other apps that may be using the microphone, such as a call.

A session stopped by itself

Sessions end at the time limit you choose in Settings, or when a phone call or another app takes over the audio. Tap Start to continue.

A translation was wrong or offensive

Touch and hold the translation and choose "Report translation". This opens a pre-filled email in your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it. (On Android, until this arrives in an update, please email us the text and the two languages.) Translations come from Google's Gemini models, so check important information, such as medical instructions, with a qualified person.

Your data

We collect nothing: no account, no analytics, no ads. Your key and your History stay on your phone. Speech and text go only to Google's Gemini API. To delete History, open History and delete one session or all. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
